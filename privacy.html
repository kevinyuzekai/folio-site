Folio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Folio is a local-first application. It does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data.

What Folio does not do

  • It makes no network requests. There is no server component.
  • It has no account system, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no analytics.
  • It contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It does not read your contacts, location, photo library (except a single image you explicitly pick for insertion), microphone, camera or health data.

What stays on your device

  • Your documents are ordinary files that you create, open and store yourself (.folio documents, or .docx files you import or export).
  • One preference is stored locally: your interface language choice. It never leaves the device.

Diagnostics

Folio writes nothing to remote logging services. Any crash reports are handled by the operating system only if you have opted in to share them with Apple, under Apple's own privacy terms — the developer receives them only in Apple's anonymised form.

Children

Folio does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
